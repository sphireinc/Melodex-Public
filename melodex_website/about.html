About Melodex

A personal music library, treated with respect.

Melodex is for listeners who want their own music collection to feel polished again: organized folders, clean metadata, album art, lyrics, track notes, and a modern player wrapped around it all.

Local-first by design.

Melodex is built around the files and folders you control. It can enrich your library, but the core experience is a desktop-grade app for browsing, repairing, and enjoying a personal collection.

  • Predictable library paths
  • Artist, album, and song browsing
  • Player, queue, lyrics, and video surfaces
  • Track profiles with metadata and enrichment

Enrichment without the mess.

Use AI-assisted metadata and public music databases to turn noisy source titles into useful library data. Melodex favors conservative, confidence-aware results over reckless guessing.

  • Metadata confidence
  • Song meaning summaries
  • Artist, album, and song trivia
  • Official links and references

Made for the music you keep.

Melodex is a library manager for music you own, created, archived, or are authorized to use. Its job is to make that collection easier to organize, enrich, play, and enjoy.

The vibe

Black glass panels, red signal accents, compact controls, dense library views, and a player that feels closer to a studio console than a social feed.

Focused

No bright clutter. The UI gets out of the way and lets the library breathe.

Useful

Metadata, lyrics, video, artwork, and notes live beside the track, not scattered across tools.

Ready to grow

The foundation leaves room for deeper library health, repair, and queue workflows over time.
